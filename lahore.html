Lahore – The City of Gardens

Where history breathes and flavours never sleep

Overview

Lahore, the capital of Pakistan's Punjab province, is a city steeped in over a thousand years of history. As the cultural and artistic heart of the nation, Lahore has been shaped by the Mughal Empire, the Sikh Empire, and British colonial rule — each leaving behind magnificent monuments, bustling bazaars, and a unique culinary tradition that draws visitors from across the globe.

Province: Punjab  |  Population: ~14 million  |  Best Season: October – March

Top Attractions

Badshahi Mosque

Built in 1673 under the reign of Mughal Emperor Aurangzeb, the Badshahi Mosque is one of the largest mosques in the world. Its vast courtyard can accommodate 100,000 worshippers. The mosque's red sandstone façade, white marble domes, and intricate frescoes are a testament to Mughal architectural mastery.

Lahore Fort (Shahi Qila)

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Lahore Fort has stood since the 11th century and was significantly expanded by Mughal emperors Akbar, Jahangir, and Shah Jahan. Highlights include the Sheesh Mahal (Palace of Mirrors), the Naulakha Pavilion, and the Diwan-i-Aam.

Food Streets

Lahore is Pakistan's undisputed culinary capital. The famous Food Street near Walled City and Gawalmandi offer legendary dishes including nihari (slow-cooked beef stew), paye (trotters), haleems, and freshly baked naan straight from tandoor ovens. The aroma of sizzling karahi and the sound of dhol drums define Lahore evenings.

Shalimar Gardens

Another UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Shalimar Gardens were laid out in 1641 by Emperor Shah Jahan. Spread over 16 hectares, the gardens feature three terraced levels, over 400 fountains, and magnificent pavilions — representing the Mughal ideal of Paradise on Earth.

Travel Tips

  • Visit Badshahi Mosque early morning to enjoy it without crowds.
  • Hire a local guide at Lahore Fort for the most enriching experience.
  • Visit Food Street after 8 PM — the atmosphere is at its peak then.
  • Book accommodation in Gulberg or Model Town for easy access to key sites.
  • Avoid peak summer months (May–July) as temperatures can reach 45°C.
