Islamabad – The Serene Capital

Where modernity meets nature in perfect harmony

Overview

Islamabad, the capital of Pakistan, is one of the few cities in the world purpose-built as a capital. Designed in the 1960s by the Greek architect Constantinos Doxiadis, Islamabad is renowned for its orderly grid layout, lush green spaces, tree-lined avenues, and the dramatic backdrop of the Margalla Hills. It consistently ranks among the cleanest and most liveable cities in South Asia.

Status: Federal Capital Territory  |  Population: ~2 million  |  Best Season: March – May & September – November

Top Attractions

Faisal Mosque

The Faisal Mosque is the most recognisable landmark in Islamabad and one of the largest mosques in the world. Designed by Turkish architect Vedat Dalokay and completed in 1986, its dramatic tent-shaped structure — flanked by four 90-metre minarets — is set against the Margalla Hills, creating one of the most photographed vistas in Pakistan.

Margalla Hills National Park

The Margalla Hills form a series of forested ridges that border Islamabad to the north, offering over 40 designated hiking trails. Trail 3 is the most popular, leading to a viewpoint with a panoramic view of the city. The national park is also home to jackals, monkeys, hedgehogs, and over 250 bird species.

Pakistan Monument

Situated on the Shakarparian Hills, the Pakistan Monument is a national monument representing the unity and solidarity of the Pakistani people. Its iconic star-and-crescent petal shape, crafted in pink granite, is illuminated beautifully at night. An adjacent museum narrates the story of Pakistan's independence movement.

Lok Virsa Museum

The Lok Virsa (Heritage) Museum preserves Pakistan's rich folk and traditional heritage. It features galleries on tribal crafts, traditional music instruments, textiles, and ceramics from across Pakistan's four provinces — an essential stop to understand the cultural depth of the nation.

Travel Tips

  • Start Trail 3 at Margalla Hills before sunrise for a cooler hike and stunning golden-hour views.
  • Visit the Faisal Mosque just after Fajr (dawn prayer) — the atmosphere is deeply serene.
  • Explore Saidpur Village for a glimpse of ancient village life within the capital's boundaries.
  • The Centaurus Mall area offers excellent dining options and is well-connected by metro.
  • Spring (March–April) is particularly beautiful when the city's cherry blossoms bloom.
